How It Works

1

Input: Emergency data is entered (Fire, Flood, etc.).

2

Packet Creation: Sender creates the structured JSON data packet.

3

Binary Conversion: Data is converted into UTF-8 bytes and then binary (0s & 1s).

4

Checksum: Sender generates the CRC-32 checksum from the data.

5

Transmission: Packet (Data + CRC) travels through the network.

6

Noise/Error: Network error may corrupt the data, flipping bits (Simulated).

7

Reception: Receiver gets the binary sequence and separates Data from CRC.

8

Recalculation: Receiver independently recalculates CRC-32 on the received Data portion.

9

Comparison: Both CRC values (Received vs Recalculated) are compared.

10

Decision: If they match, ACCEPT packet. If they differ, REJECT packet (corruption detected).
